I am an Assistant Professor in the group of Rüdiger Frey at the [Institute for Statistics and Mathematics](https://www.wu.ac.at/en/statmath) at WU Vienna, and an associate member of the [Emerging Risks Research Centre](https://www.mq.edu.au/research/research-centres-institutes-and-initiatives/emerging-risks) at Macquarie Business School.

Previously, I held a postdoctoral appointment in the group of Patrick Cheridito in the Department of Mathematics at ETH Zurich, where I was affiliated with the [FinsureTech Hub](https://finsuretech.ethz.ch) and associated with the [ETH AI Center](https://ai.ethz.ch).

I received my doctorate from TU Wien and Macquarie University under the supervision of Uwe Schmock and Pavel V. Shevchenko, graduating [sub auspiciis Praesidentis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sub_auspiciis_Praesidentis) at TU Wien.

My research lies at the intersection of applied mathematics, mathematical finance, and actuarial science, with related work in machine learning and climate economics.
